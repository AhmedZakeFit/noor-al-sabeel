Prayer times app features

Prayer times

Accurate times for the five daily prayers and sunrise, today and tomorrow, with a countdown to the next prayer. Twelve calculation methods including Umm al-Qura, the Muslim World League and the Egyptian authority, plus a ±30-minute shift on any time to match your mosque — all offline.

Full adhan

A complete recorded adhan plays at prayer time even with the screen locked; Fajr uses its own dawn adhan with the extra dawn phrase. The recordings are bundled with the app and work offline from the first install.

Pre-prayer reminder

A configurable reminder before each prayer with a live countdown in the notification that seamlessly becomes the prayer alert when the time arrives, with per-prayer sound and vibration choices.

Qibla compass

A precise compass with magnetic-declination correction showing the qibla bearing and the distance to the Kaaba from your location, with calibration guidance and a live accuracy indicator.

Hijri calendar

The Hijri and Gregorian dates together, using the official Umm al-Qura table (1924–2076), plus midnight and the last third of the night, with a manual day adjustment for local moon sighting.

Full privacy

No accounts, ads or tracking; your data stays on your device — the internet is used only for the optional online city search and update checks. The app speaks Arabic and English, with light and dark themes.

How to install and use

Download the app file

Tap the download button above — the app file (APK) downloads immediately and shows up in the download notification or your Downloads folder.

Open the file and install

Open the file from the notification or your Downloads folder and tap Install. If Android asks for permission to “install unknown apps”, grant it once for your browser and confirm the installation.

Set your location

On first launch pick a city from the bundled catalog or use your current location — you land straight on the prayer times.

Enable alerts

Grant the notification permission when asked and choose your alert style: a regular notification or the full adhan, plus a pre-prayer reminder.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All prayer-time, qibla and Hijri-calendar calculations run on your device, and a bundled catalog of about 4,300 cities (2,088 with Arabic names) works with no internet at all. The app uses the network for only two optional features: the online city search and the update check.

Is it really free and ad-free?

Yes. Noor Al-Sabeel is completely free: no ads, no subscriptions, no accounts and no tracking. The code is open source under the Apache-2.0 license — you can inspect it or build the app yourself from the official repository.

How does the qibla compass work?

With a built-in compass that applies magnetic-declination correction and shows the qibla bearing and the distance to the Kaaba from your location, along with calibration guidance and a live accuracy indicator. Consumer phone compasses have real physical limits, so when in doubt double-check against other landmarks.

Which calculation methods are available?

Twelve methods: Umm al-Qura (with the Ramadan isha rule), the Muslim World League, the Egyptian General Authority of Survey (the default), ISNA, Karachi, Gulf, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Turkey, France, and fully custom angles — and any individual time can be shifted by up to ±30 minutes to match your mosque.

How do I install it on my phone?

Tap the “Download the app (APK)” button at the top of this page to fetch the latest stable signed release directly, then open the file, grant your browser the “install unknown apps” permission once and confirm. No Google Play account is needed, and after installation the app checks for updates by itself.

Can I match the times with my local mosque?

Yes. You can shift any individual prayer time by up to 30 minutes earlier or later to align it with your mosque's adhan. Note that the app does not search for nearby mosques; the adjustment is manual, per prayer.

Is it available on Google Play or F-Droid?

Not on Google Play at the moment; the download is a direct signed APK from GitHub Releases, with a published SHA-256 checksum for every file. An F-Droid submission is planned, but acceptance is up to the F-Droid maintainers and is not guaranteed.

Your privacy is a trust

The app collects nothing and sends nothing to any server; settings and location stay on your device. Read the full privacy policy, written against the actual source code.
